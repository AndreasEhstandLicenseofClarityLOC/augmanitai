How do we describe what happens between a person and an AI? How does a thought become an image? What evidence would make an interpretation worth trusting?

These questions provide starting points for a conversation about a paid exploratory project, a workshop or a research collaboration. The materials below let you inspect the starting point before making an enquiry.

## Three starting points

| Your question | Public material to inspect | Possible project to discuss |

These are possible engagements, not reports of completed client projects or validated outcomes. The conceptual paper and codebook have not been empirically validated. A repository deposit or DOI establishes a reference to a work, not independent endorsement.

## Make the first conversation concrete

Send a short description of:

- the problem and who needs the result;
- one example you are allowed to share;
- the decision or deliverable you want;
- your timeframe and whether the enquiry concerns research, a workshop or a paid pilot.

[Contact Andreas Ehstand on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andreas-ehstand/). Start with a non-confidential outline. Scope, fees, evaluation criteria and any necessary confidentiality arrangements can be agreed before a project begins. Public materials retain their stated licences; an enquiry grants no additional rights.
